Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Sentinel is a personal safety app. Here is how we handle your data.

What we collect

We collect your email address to authenticate you, your display name to identify you in alert emails, the email addresses and names of your emergency contacts, your GPS location while you have an active event, any files you choose to attach to an event, and your event data including names, messages, and timer durations.

Your location

Location is collected approximately every 30 seconds only while you have an active event running. We store only your most recent position, not a movement history. When your last active event ends, location collection stops immediately. Location is shared with your emergency contacts in alert emails as a map link.

What we do not collect

We do not collect analytics, usage data, advertising identifiers, or any data from your phone contacts, camera, or microphone. There are no ads, no trackers, and no third-party analytics in the app.

How we use your data

If you do not check in before your event timer expires, we send an alert email to your emergency contacts. The email includes your name, your message, your last known location, and any files you attached. That is the only purpose of the data we collect.

We do not sell, share, or provide your data to anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

Data storage

Your data is stored on servers in the United States.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual events from your history. You can delete saved contacts at any time. You can delete your entire account and all associated data permanently from the settings screen. Deletion is irreversible.

Staged files not attached to an event are automatically deleted after 24 hours.

Children

Sentinel is not intended for anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
